Everything You Need to Manage Training

SkillBridge provides a centralized system for managing employees, trainers, courses, assessments, skills, results and certificates.

📚

Training Programs

Create, update and manage professional training programs with descriptions, duration and required skills.

👨‍🏫

Trainer Management

Assign trainers to training programs and allow trainers to manage assessments and monitor employee performance.

📈

Progress Tracking

Employees can update their course progress and easily monitor their learning journey from their dashboard.

📝

MCQ Assessments

Trainers can create multiple-choice assessments and employees can complete them online.

Automatic Results

Assessment scores, percentages and pass/fail status are calculated automatically.

Digital Certificates

Successful employees can receive downloadable PDF certificates and verify their achievements.
